- iozone-musl: 4,096 paired 1 KiB pwrite samples
- iozone-glibc: 4,096 paired 1 KiB pwrite samples
- Aggregate below: 8,192 paired samples (the two ABI-equivalent 4,096-call runs).

### PageCache::write_user level

### ext4 write_at_user level
- fs_arc + page_cache setup: 7483.91 cycles (0.70%)
- cache.write_user: 1060337.93 cycles (99.21%)
- logical_size + retain: 973.53 cycles (0.09%)

### Interpretation
- `cache.write_user` dominates the ext4-level interval. This includes the mandatory nested `[pc-write-timing]` console line, so its absolute value is intentionally console-contaminated.
- The PageCache sub-stage split excludes that final console line: the 1 KiB UserBuffer copy is the largest measured data-path component, followed by lookup/lease; valid+commit and dirty balancing are small.
- The pwrite-level `File::pwrite_user` interval likewise contains nested ext4/PageCache diagnostic output. Use it as a call-chain attribution boundary, not a production-cost estimate.
