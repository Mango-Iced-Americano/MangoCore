=== ROOT CAUSE ===
apply_current_umask() at os/src/syscall/fs/common.rs:241 was doing:
  mode & S_IALLUGO & ~umask
This strips S_IFMT type bits (S_IFIFO=0o010000). After umask, mode lost S_IFIFO,
causing ext4 mknod() to receive mode without type bits → EINVAL.

FIX: Preserve S_IFMT bits, apply umask only to permission bits:
  let type_bits = mode & S_IFMT;
  let perm_bits = mode & S_IALLUGO;
  type_bits | (perm_bits & !umask)
